## Company Overview
Walmart Inc. is a multinational retail corporation operating in the **retail industry**. The company provides groceries, household goods, electronics, 
apparel, and a wide range of consumer products through both physical stores and online platforms. 

## Why Ratio Analysis Matters
Ratio analysis is a critical tool for evaluating a company’s financial health because it converts raw financial statement data into performance data. 
While the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement provide numerical values, ratios make it easier to assess trends and compare performance over time. 
Ratio analysis helps reveal insights into profitability, operational efficiency, debt usage, and liquidity. 
It also supports benchmarking against industry competitors and assists management in strategic decision-making.

## Six Ratio Categories
The analysis will focus on the following six ratio categories:

- **Performance Ratios** – Measure how effectively the company generates returns from assets and equity.
- **Profitability Ratios** – Evaluate the company’s ability to generate profit from revenue.
- **Efficiency Ratios** – Assess how efficiently assets, inventory, and receivables are used to produce sales.
- **Leverage Ratios** – Examine the company’s use of debt financing and long-term solvency.
- **Liquidity Ratios** – Measure the ability to meet short-term financial obligations.
- **Du Pont Analysis** – Breaks return on equity into profit margin, asset turnover, and leverage components.
